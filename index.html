Privacy policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Information you provide

When you submit an inquiry, Gathered collects your name, email address, event type, and the preferred contact details you choose to provide. You may also share an event date, venue or city, theme, and additional details. Please do not include sensitive personal information that is not needed for your inquiry.

How information is used

Your information is used to review and respond to your inquiry, discuss your celebration website, and send the confirmation email you requested. Preferred contact details are used only to contact you by the method you selected.

Storage and service providers

Inquiry details are stored in a Google spreadsheet and processed using Google Apps Script and Google email services to deliver the confirmation. Google processes information under its own privacy terms. Gathered does not sell your personal information.

Retention and your choices

Inquiry information is kept only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, provide requested services, and maintain appropriate business records. You may ask to access, correct, or delete your inquiry information by replying to your confirmation email. Some information may need to be retained where required for legitimate business or legal purposes.

Security and external resources

Reasonable steps are taken to protect inquiry information, but no method of online transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed completely secure. This website loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which may receive standard connection information when the page is viewed.

Updates and questions

This policy may be updated as the website or inquiry process changes. For privacy questions or a request about your information, reply to a Gathered confirmation email.
